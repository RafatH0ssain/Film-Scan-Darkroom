    Measuring the film base

    The base is the unexposed rebate between frames. Measuring it beats the automatic guess, which assumes the densest part of the picture is close to base. The area you mark is re-measured at full resolution on export, so a small on-screen rectangle is enough.

    About the film settings

    These drive both the preview and the saved positive, so the file matches what you judged here. The scan itself is never modified.

    About unsharp masking

    Sharpness is added by subtracting a blurred copy of the luminance. Amount sets the strength, radius the scale of the edges, and threshold keeps low-contrast grain from being sharpened along with detail.

    About presets

    A preset stores the film and sharpen settings of this frame, matched by the roll's film stock the next time that stock is opened. Crop and angle are per frame and are never shared.
